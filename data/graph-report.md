## 9. Three findings for whoever designs the demo route

Written by hand on 2026-11-21 from the figures of that day's build (sections 4, 6 and 7). The build appends this text unchanged, so check the figures above if the data has been downloaded again.

1. **Akropoli station → Thiseio station along Dionysiou Areopagitou and Apostolou Pavlou is the route the data supports best.** The two streets form one continuous line in the graph: 1571 m, of which 1520 m are `pedestrian` edges, with no road crossing on the way and metro entrances mapped at both ends (3 at Akropoli, 2 at Thiseio). Two cautions. The shortest path by length does not follow the walk: it cuts through Plaka on `shared_road` streets (1512 m) or, without roads, over the footways of the Acropolis south slope (1644 m, with steps), which is a ticketed site that OSM does not mark as closed. The routing engine needs a cost that prefers `pedestrian` edges, and the demo should fix the walk as the expected result. And no edge of the walk has `tactile_paving` tagged; the surface is `sett`.

2. **Monastiraki station → City Hall along Athinas has no usable pavement or crossing data.** The shortest path is 526 m, all of it on the centre line of Athinas (`shared_road`). Separate pavements are mapped for at most 41% of the two sides and do not connect into a line; no `footway=crossing` way exists in the Athinas corridor, only 3 crossing nodes, all without signals; the City Hall (way 768849988) has no mapped entrance, so its place is snapped to the nearest node on Athinas from its address tag (`entranceVerified: false`). ION cannot say on this street which side the person is on, or announce a crossing as an edge. The only path that avoids street centre lines is 1335 m, over the pedestrian streets east of Athinas (Aiolou) and into Kotzia Square: longer, but every metre is mapped foot infrastructure. Choose deliberately which of the two the demo shows.

3. **Signals are well mapped, audible signals and tactile paving are not, and half of the crossings are only nodes.** Of 169 crossing ways, signals are known for 98.8%, but `traffic_signals:sound=yes` appears on 9 ways and 2 further nodes (8 of the ways at Syntagma, one on Stadiou, the nodes at Irodou Attikou and Omonoia), and tactile paving is known for 2.4%. 213 of the 424 `highway=crossing` nodes have no crossing way through them, so the graph has no "crossing" edge there and the contract cannot carry their facts (they are in `athens-centre.extras.json`, `crossingNodes`). A demo that should announce an audible crossing must pass Syntagma; anywhere else the honest announcement is "crossing, signal sound unknown". The routing engine should also merge the two halves of a crossing way (it is cut at the road centre line) into one announcement.
